Your computers

The team's computers you may reach. Open them in the SSH Remote app, signed in to the team (Settings, Team).

Devices

The computers that joined the team. Online: seen in the last 3 minutes.

Add computers

Users

The people who sign in to the team: in this console, and in the app to reach its computers.

Groups

Groups of users, for access rules that name many people at once. Add people to a group in their account.

Device groups

Groups of computers. Each keeps a policy: the settings its computers take from the team, over their own.

Access rules

Who may reach which computers, with what rights, and whether the person at the screen is asked first. A device group's policy can take rights away again.

    Enrollment

    A computer joins the team with a token: run its installer line on the computer. A token puts the computers into a device group, works until it expires, and for as many computers as it allows.

    Audit log

    Who came in where, when they left, who was refused, and the files sent, as the computers report them.

    Settings

    What the app shows of the team, the relay its computers use, and how people sign in.

    The team

    Shown in the app and here.

    Logo

    PNG, SVG or JPEG, at most 256 KB. Square looks best.

    The relay the team's computers use, as HOST or HOST:PORT. Empty: each keeps its own.

    Single sign-on (OpenID Connect)

    People sign in through the organization's identity provider, in the console and in the app.

    Directory (LDAP)
