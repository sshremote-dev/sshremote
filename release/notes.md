- Films are sharper than RustDesk's for fewer bytes. Measured on one computer, each viewer's own pictures of the same film compared with the film itself: RustDesk 1.5.3 39.9-40.6 dB at 8.0 kbit a picture, SSH Remote 41.3 dB at 5.4 kbit (0.25.38 spent 10.4 kbit for 43.7 dB). The whole bench (still screen, typing, scrolling, pages, film) took 1,124 KB against RustDesk's 2,190 KB, less in every part of it.
- A film's whole area always goes as a film. Its area grows when the film turns out larger than its first frames showed, and moves when a page that kept changing as the film began was taken with part of it. Before, the rest of such a film could go as exact pictures, frame after frame: up to 3 MB a second instead of 20 KB.

Install with one line (`irm https://sshremote.dev/install.ps1 | iex` in PowerShell, `curl -fsSL https://sshremote.dev/install.sh | sh` on macOS and Linux), or download the file for your system below. An installed copy updates itself with `sshremote update`. `SHA256SUMS` lists every file's SHA-256, as does the signed [list](https://sshremote.dev/download/latest.json) the program updates from.
