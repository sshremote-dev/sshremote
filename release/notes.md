- The viewer draws a remote screen with a third of the work: no background is painted under the picture any more (only around it), and a picture shown at its own size is taken texel for texel. On a computer without a GPU (a virtual machine, a remote desktop) this was most of the cost: there a film now shows at its full 30 pictures a second for 1.5 cores instead of 5, as evenly as RustDesk's viewer (gaps of 33 ms, 38 ms at the 90th percentile, against 36 ms).
- Windows: the window opens on computers whose graphics driver crashes in Direct3D 12 (0.25.40, 0.25.41): it starts again on OpenGL, and tries Direct3D 12 once where OpenGL finds no adapter.

Install with one line (`irm https://sshremote.dev/install.ps1 | iex` in PowerShell, `curl -fsSL https://sshremote.dev/install.sh | sh` on macOS and Linux), or download the file for your system below. An installed copy updates itself with `sshremote update`. `SHA256SUMS` lists every file's SHA-256, as does the signed [list](https://sshremote.dev/download/latest.json) the program updates from.
