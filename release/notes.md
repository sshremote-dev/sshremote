- Choose the codec films come in, in the screen's display menu (or `--codec`): Auto, AV1, H.264, or H.265. H.265 is encoded on the host's video hardware (Media Foundation on Windows, VideoToolbox on macOS, GStreamer's VA-API, NVENC and Quick Sync encoders on Linux) and decoded by the viewer's own system (Media Foundation with the HEVC Video Extensions on Windows, VideoToolbox on macOS, GStreamer on Linux). A codec either computer cannot use shows greyed, with the reason. The choice is kept for new connections.

Install with one line (`irm https://sshremote.dev/install.ps1 | iex` in PowerShell, `curl -fsSL https://sshremote.dev/install.sh | sh` on macOS and Linux), or download the file for your system below. An installed copy updates itself with `sshremote update`. `SHA256SUMS` lists every file's SHA-256, as does the signed [list](https://sshremote.dev/download/latest.json) the program updates from.
